How a round works

Each question has several answers. The top tells you how many of them are correct. Tap every answer you think is right, then tap “Check answer”. Then you see the solution:

Sample question

Choose 2 correct answers.

✓Correct and selected by you: green
✕Wrong, but selected by you: red
Correct, but not selected: dashed green
Wrong and not selected: stays unmarked

Just an example, nothing here can be tapped.

Note: the questions are being translated step by step. Questions that are not translated yet appear in German.

Scoring

You only get a point if exactly the correct answers are selected: all correct ones and no wrong one. This is deliberately strict so you really master the material.

Ending

You can stop any time with “End” at the top right or “End now” after checking. The result counts only the questions you answered.

On the quiz start page

  • Knowledge level: The percentage of questions you last answered correctly, overall and per category. Questions you have never answered count as not known.
  • Shuffle all questions: All questions in random order.
  • Exam: 20 random questions.
  • Category: Practice only questions from one category.
  • Practice mistakes: The questions you last got wrong. After the result you can also repeat wrong questions right away.
  • Reset progress: At the very bottom, if you want to start from zero.

Install as an app

This is optional. First open the quiz with “Start quiz” and add it from there, otherwise you will land on this page again later.

  • Android (Chrome): Menu at the top right (three dots), then “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”.
  • iPhone (Safari): Share icon, then “Add to Home Screen”.
  • iPhone (Chrome): Share icon next to the address bar, then “Add to Home Screen”.

Menu names may differ slightly depending on the version.

Your progress

Knowledge level, mistakes list and best score are stored only on your device in the browser. Nobody else sees them. Always use the same way in, either the app icon or the browser. If you clear the website data, your progress is gone.
